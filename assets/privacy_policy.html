Privacy Policy

Flettra — Your Travel Community

Welcome to Flettra ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Flettra mobile application. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with the terms, please discontinue use of the app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo.
  • Ride information: Origin, destination, travel dates, vehicle type, estimated budget per seat.
  • Posts and media: Photos, text content you share in the feed.
  • Payment information: Subscription plan details processed via Razorpay. We do not store card numbers — payments are handled by Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

We collect information automatically:

  • Location data: Real-time GPS coordinates while a ride is active and you have granted location permission. Used solely for live group tracking and nearby POI search.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, push notification token (FCM).
  • Usage data: Features used, pages viewed, crash reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your Flettra account.
  • Enable ride creation, discovery, and group coordination.
  • Show your live location to ride group members during an active ride (only while the ride is ongoing and you have enabled location sharing).
  • Display nearby points of interest (fuel, stays, mechanics) relevant to your route.
  • Generate AI-powered trip itineraries based on your ride details.
  • Send push notifications for ride updates, join requests, and community activity.
  • Process subscription payments.
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs.

3. Location Data

Flettra requests precise location (GPS) permission. Location is used:

  • During live rides to share your position with your ride group.
  • To search for nearby points of interest (fuel stations, hotels, mechanics).

Location is not collected in the background when the app is closed or a ride is not active. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as follows:

  • With ride participants: Your name, profile photo, and live location are visible to members of rides you join.
  • Service providers: Razorpay (payments), Firebase (push notifications), Google Maps (mapping), OpenStreetMap/Nominatim (POI search), Anthropic Claude API (AI itinerary generation).
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers hosted in India. We use HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and industry-standard security practices for data at rest. No system is 100% secure; use strong passwords and keep your device secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing privacy@flettra.com. Deletion is completed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Flettra is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data via your profile settings.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for location sharing at any time.
  • Opt out of marketing notifications via app settings.

9. Third-Party Services

The app integrates third-party services with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps
  • Razorpay
  • Firebase (Google)
  • Anthropic Claude
  • OpenStreetMap

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of Flettra after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
